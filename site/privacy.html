Scope and contact

适用范围与联系

This policy describes Jarney / 游点钱, developed by Yadong Hu. Contact 0@hugohu.top about privacy or data management.

本政策适用于 Yadong Hu 开发的 Jarney／游点钱。有关隐私或数据管理，请联系 0@hugohu.top。

Savings records and iCloud

储蓄记录与 iCloud

The app stores contribution amounts, dates, ownership, confirmation status, imported transaction details, travel goals, saving rules, card history, preferences, and recaps on your device and in Apple CloudKit. CloudKit account identifiers associate records with your iCloud account. If you share a fund, invited participants can access its shared records. Jarney does not ask for your Apple Account password or bank password.

应用在设备和 Apple CloudKit 中保存贡献金额、日期、归属、确认状态、导入的交易详情、旅行目标、存钱规则、卡片历史、偏好与回顾。CloudKit 账号标识用于关联你的 iCloud 账号。共享基金后，受邀参与者可以访问共享记录。游点钱不会索取 Apple 账号密码或银行密码。

Optional Wallet import

可选 Wallet 导入

Apple’s FinanceKit transaction selector lets you choose records to import. Only the selected records returned by Apple are imported. Those financial details become part of the fund history and may sync through CloudKit and be visible to your invited partner. The app does not continuously read your entire Wallet or move money.

Apple FinanceKit 交易选择界面让你主动选取记录。应用仅导入 Apple 返回的所选交易。这些财务详情成为基金历史的一部分，可通过 CloudKit 同步并由受邀旅伴查看。应用不会持续读取整个 Wallet，也不执行转账。

Optional location

可选定位

With permission, location is used on the device to calculate the straight-line distance to a destination reference point. GPS coordinates are not saved or uploaded by Jarney. A coarse derived distance is cached locally for that destination. You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings; Jarney clears that location cache when it detects revocation.

获得授权后，应用在设备上计算到目的地参考点的直线距离。游点钱不保存或上传 GPS 坐标，仅在本地缓存对应目的地的粗粒度距离。你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销权限；应用检测到撤销后会清除定位缓存。

Market data and external links

市场数据与外部链接

When market tracking is enabled, requests to Yahoo Finance include the selected market symbol and requested time range. The provider also receives standard network information such as your IP address. Contribution records and iCloud pairing codes are not included in those requests. Opening landmark source links or contacting support uses the services you choose and their privacy policies.

启用市场跟踪后，向 Yahoo Finance 发送的请求包含选定证券代码和查询时间范围。提供方也会收到 IP 地址等常规网络信息；请求不包含贡献记录或 iCloud 配对码。打开景点资料链接或联系支持时，相关服务按各自隐私政策处理数据。

Diagnostics and tracking

诊断与跟踪

The current app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Operational diagnostics are written locally through Apple’s logging system. If you voluntarily send screenshots or logs to support, they may contain personal information; review and redact them first. Apple may separately process TestFlight diagnostics under its own policies.

当前应用没有广告或第三方分析 SDK。运行诊断通过 Apple 日志系统在本地记录。如果你主动向支持发送截图或日志，其中可能含有个人信息，请先核对并遮挡。Apple 可能按其政策另行处理 TestFlight 诊断数据。

Retention, controls, and deletion

保留、控制与删除

Records remain on your device and in CloudKit until removed through data-management actions. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete iCloud records, and may remove local changes that have not synchronized. Invited partners may retain information already shared with them. Contact support for deletion assistance; shared-data deletion should be coordinated with the other participant.

记录保留在设备和 CloudKit 中，直到通过数据管理操作删除。卸载应用不会自动删除 iCloud 记录，并可能丢失尚未同步的本地修改。受邀旅伴可能保留已共享的信息。需要删除协助时请联系支持；共享数据的删除应与另一位参与者协商。

This website

本网站

This website uses no advertising or analytics scripts. It stores your language choice in browser localStorage. The hosting server or HTTPS provider may retain ordinary request metadata, such as IP address, time, and requested page, for operation and security. The website does not receive your in-app fund records.

本网站没有广告或分析脚本，仅在浏览器 localStorage 保存语言选择。托管服务器或 HTTPS 服务提供方可能为运行与安全保留 IP 地址、请求时间、页面路径等常规请求信息。网站不会接收你的应用内基金记录。
