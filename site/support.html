iCloud and a partner

iCloud 与旅伴同步

Use the iCloud account signed into your device. Invite your partner through the app’s iCloud sharing controls. Updates save locally and synchronize in the background. For connection issues, check iCloud status and the app’s sync settings before reinstalling.

使用设备上已登录的 iCloud 账号，在应用的 iCloud 同步设置中邀请旅伴。修改先保存到本地，再后台同步；遇到问题时先检查 iCloud 状态和同步设置，不要急于卸载。

Wallet selection

Wallet 交易选择

Wallet import is optional. Apple’s selector only returns transactions you choose and may be unavailable for some accounts or devices. It does not provide a complete account balance or transfer money. Manual recording remains available.

Wallet 导入是可选功能。Apple 选择界面仅返回你主动选中的交易，部分账户或设备可能没有可选交易；它不提供完整账户余额，也不能转账。你仍可手动记录。

Records and confirmation

记录与到账确认

Record the correct person, amount, and date. A pending deposit does not advance the fund until confirmed. Before retrying an operation, check history to avoid duplicate recording.

记录时核对归属人、金额和日期。待到账记录只有确认后才推进基金。重复操作前先检查历史，避免重复记账。

Managing or deleting data

管理与删除数据

Shared fund records may be visible to the partner you invite. Removing the app alone does not delete CloudKit data. Contact support for deletion assistance; clearly identify the fund without sending secrets. Deleting shared records can affect both participants.

共享基金记录可由你邀请的旅伴查看。仅卸载应用不会删除 CloudKit 数据。如需删除协助，请联系支持并说明基金，不要发送密钥或配对码；删除共享记录可能影响双方。
