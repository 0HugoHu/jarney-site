Currently in development

目前仍在开发

Jarney is a savings journal: it does not hold funds or transfer money. English and Simplified Chinese are supported. TestFlight access requires an invitation when a beta is available.

游点钱记录储蓄活动，不托管资金，也不执行银行转账。支持中文与英文；测试版可用后，通过邀请加入 TestFlight。
